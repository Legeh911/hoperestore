Know Before You Go

Understanding Your Copay

Your copay is determined by your individual insurance plan. We recommend contacting your insurance provider directly before your first session to confirm your mental health benefits, copay amount, and any deductible that may apply.

Questions to ask your insurance provider

  • 1. Do I have outpatient mental health benefits?
  • 2. What is my copay for telehealth therapy sessions?
  • 3. Have I met my deductible for this plan year?
  • 4. Do I need a referral to see a therapist?

Out-of-Pocket Options

Self-Pay Rates

We welcome clients who prefer to pay out of pocket. Our self-pay rates are straightforward and transparent no hidden fees, no surprises.

Service Duration Rate
Initial Intake Session Comprehensive first appointment your goals, history & care plan 60 min $175
Individual Therapy Session Ongoing one-on-one telehealth sessions tailored to your needs 60 min $150
Self-pay clients are billed at the time of service. Payment is accepted via all major credit and debit cards, as well as HSA/FSA cards.

A Note on Accessibility

We believe that cost should not be the only thing standing between you and the support you need. If you have questions or concerns about affordability, we encourage you to reach out we're happy to have an honest conversation about what options may be available to you. Everyone deserves access to compassionate, quality mental health care.
