Healing is more
powerful in community

Our structured 8-week group therapy program is designed for adults who are ready to understand their anxiety and depression more deeply and build real, practical skills to manage them.

Led by a licensed therapist, this virtual group combines psychoeducation, evidence-based coping strategies, and the power of shared experience in a safe, confidential environment.

What This Group Covers

  • Understand how anxiety and depression affect the brain and body
  • Identify your personal triggers, thought patterns, and emotional cycles
  • Use grounding and regulation skills to calm your mind and nervous system
  • Challenge negative thoughts and reduce rumination
  • Improve motivation and daily functioning
  • Build healthier routines and habits
  • Strengthen communication and boundary-setting skills
  • Create a personalized coping plan for life after the group

"You don't have to figure it all out alone."

Who Is This
Group For?

This group is designed for adults (18+) who are experiencing any of the following challenges and are ready to learn, grow, and heal alongside others.

01

Anxiety

Persistent worry, racing thoughts, panic episodes, or a nervous system that feels constantly on edge.

02

Depression

Low mood, persistent sadness, loss of energy, or difficulty finding meaning and motivation in daily life.

03

Stress & Overwhelm

Feeling stretched too thin, overwhelmed by responsibilities, or unable to decompress and rest.

04

Emotional Dysregulation

Difficulty managing emotional responses, frequent mood swings, or reactions that feel out of proportion.

05

Low Motivation

Struggling to get through daily tasks, feeling stuck, or losing interest in things that once mattered to you.

06

Negative Thought Patterns

Inner criticism, self-doubt, catastrophizing, or thoughts that spiral into unhelpful or harmful territory.

The Program

Week by Week
Breakdown

Each session builds on the last creating a progressive, skills-based journey from understanding your symptoms to building a lasting coping plan.

Week 1
Introduction

Building Safety & Setting the Foundation

Overview of group purpose, expectations, and confidentiality. Introductions and beginning to build trust within the group.

Week 2
Psychoeducation

Understanding Anxiety & Depression

How anxiety and depression affect the brain and body. Learn about cognitive distortions, the anxiety cycle, and the depression cycle. Begin building your skills toolbox.

Week 3
Self-Awareness

Identifying Triggers & Emotional Patterns

Recognize internal and external triggers, early warning signs, and emotional patterns. Learn grounding skills for managing difficult moments in real time.

Week 4
Regulation

Emotional Regulation Skills

Techniques to calm the mind and body, including breathing exercises and somatic regulation. Understand the Window of Tolerance and how to stay within it.

Week 5
Cognitive Skills

Challenging Negative Thoughts

Learn cognitive-behavioral strategies to reframe negative thinking and reduce rumination. Practice thought-challenging and develop personalized coping statements.

Week 6
Behavioral Activation

Increasing Motivation & Building Routines

Behavioral activation skills to improve mood and motivation. Develop small, achievable goals and build supportive daily routines that sustain progress.

Week 7
Relationships

Strengthening Communication & Boundaries

Understand how anxiety and depression impact relationships. Learn assertive communication and healthy boundary-setting strategies for home and work.

Week 8
Graduation

Creating Your Personalized Coping Plan

Review the skills learned throughout the program. Build a long-term coping plan to maintain progress and continue thriving beyond the group.
