Our Practice

Founded by Doreen Boateng, M.Ed, LCPC, NCC, Hope Restored Counseling Services is a telehealth group practice serving adults throughout Virginia, Maryland, and Washington DC. We specialize in Christian counseling, trauma-informed care, and holistic mental health support — because we believe healing happens when clinical excellence meets faith.
